# Design Thinking & Audience

## Audience and Intent

**Audience:** Ethiopian youth and young adults chasing ambitious dreams, and the mothers who sacrifice for them.
**Intent:** To show that success is never achieved alone. The invisible support and advice of a mother are the true foundation of a child's dream.

## User Test 1: The Radio Cut

**Method:** Played the audio + subtitles over a black screen to 3 family members.
**Feedback:** They loved the pacing and the emotional weight of the mother's advice. However, they noted the Gemini TTS voice sounded slightly robotic and had an English accent speaking Amharic.
**Change:** We completely swapped the TTS engine to Addis AI (`am-hamen`), which provided a native, emotional Amharic voice that completely transformed the film.

## User Test 2: The Near-Final Cut

**Method:** Showed the assembled Ken Burns video to the same 3 people.
**Feedback:** The visual storytelling was clear, but they missed the "motion" of a real film.
**Change:** We attempted to use Wan 2.2 for full motion. However, due to GPU memory limits, we intentionally pivoted to a highly polished "cinematic motion-photo" style (Ken Burns) which gave it a documentary feel. We added subtle ambient room tone to make it feel alive.
