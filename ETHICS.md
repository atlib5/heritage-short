# Ethics & Consent

## Consent

The film is based on a true story about my mother. I have obtained her explicit consent to use her story and likeness for this educational project. No real human voice was cloned; AI TTS (Addis AI) was used.

## Labelling

The film ends with a visible AI disclosure card. The public post will use the platform's built-in AI label.
